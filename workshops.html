Your ticket includes

Everything you need

  • Hands-on, step by step guidance from Tara
  • Your own jar of Lady Whistledough, Tara's 50 year old starter
  • Mix and fold your own dough to bake at home the next day
  • Tasting of fresh sourdough samples
  • All ingredients and supplies, and a take-home recipe booklet
  • No experience needed. Come solo or bring a friend

Good to know

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself. Everything is provided.

Are drinks included?

Yes, every guest is welcomed with a Christmas mocktail on arrival.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, the ticket price is refunded if you cancel at least 7 days before the workshop.

Is it suitable for dietary restrictions?

Classes use wheat flour and aren't suitable for guests with celiac disease or a wheat allergy.

Rather have your own night?

Gather your friends and Tara will bring the workshop to your home. $65 per person, and the host joins for free.
